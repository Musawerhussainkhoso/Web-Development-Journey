About MUET

Mehran University of Engineering and Technology (MUET), Jamshoro, is one of Pakistan's leading engineering universities. Established in 1976, MUET offers high-quality education, modern laboratories, experienced faculty members, and research opportunities in different engineering fields.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized university by producing innovative engineers, researchers, and leaders who contribute to society through knowledge and technology.

Our Mission

  • Provide quality engineering education.
  • Promote innovation and research.
  • Develop professional and ethical graduates.
  • Serve society through technology.
